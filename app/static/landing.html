Questions

Good to know

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You paste one line into your website, usually in a settings box. If you get stuck I will help.

Which AI models can it use?

Claude, GPT, Llama (via Groq), Grok (via xAI) and OpenRouter. You can use your own AI key or the included ones.

How is my data protected?

API keys are encrypted, the assistant is built not to reveal its instructions or dump your documents, and the chat can be limited to your own domains.

How does billing work?

Monthly, arranged directly with me. Nothing is charged automatically.

What happens to visitor chats?

You can read them in your dashboard, delete them any time, and they are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Why can't I just sign up?

So your keys and content stay safe and I can set everything up for your business. Message me and I will create your profile.
